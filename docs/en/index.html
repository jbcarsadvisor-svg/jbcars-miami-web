Do I need to know which car I want?+

No. Start with how you'll use it, the space you need and your approximate budget. The first conversation helps guide your search.

Are the cars in the photos available?+

The photographs feature JB Cars content and community references, not a current inventory. Ask Jesús about available options, prices and terms.

Can I ask about financing?+

You can discuss your questions with Jesús. Any financing, approval, rate or term depends on the relevant lender's evaluation. Approval is not guaranteed. Do not send financial documents through this form.

How do I send my inquiry?+

Complete the form to prepare a message and review it. Then open WhatsApp Business or your email application and send it yourself. You can also call directly. Preparing a message does not mean it has been sent.

Is JB Cars an official dealership website?+

No. JB Cars is an independent advisory website operated by F K BOUQUET CORPORATION. It is not the official website of Toyota of Hollywood. Confirm the scope and any costs of a service with Jesús before engaging it.
